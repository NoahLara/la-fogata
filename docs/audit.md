# Repository audit

Date: 2026-10-05. Scope: `apps/web`, `apps/realtime`, `packages/shared`, root config. Method: four read-only reviews (scene and design; components, app and preferences; data, i18n, sound, fire and config; realtime, shared and monorepo), each finding then checked adversarially (read the surrounding code, grepped usages, tried to disprove it). Items that could not be confirmed were dropped; those that were only partly confirmed say "medium confidence".

Size: S under about 1 hour, M a few hours, L a day or more.

**Headline: no critical findings.** No `any`, no non-null `!`, no import cycles at runtime, and the burden text never reaches an event, a service, storage, a log or the scene. Demo code is not in the production build. The important findings are a weak risk filter, focus and error handling gaps, a scene lifecycle race and per-frame allocations, and three files that mix too much.

None.
