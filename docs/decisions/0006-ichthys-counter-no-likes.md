# 0006. The ichthys counter: no likes, no ranking

Status: accepted

**Context.** People leave petitions on hard nights. Metrics that compare or reward would hurt them.

**Decision.** The only response is the ichthys, meaning "I'm with you", with one counter (`prayers`), once per star per session, hidden at 0. There are no likes, no ranking, no streaks, no author information. The sky shows petitions with fewer prayers first so none goes unaccompanied. The author hears only "Alguien te acompaña." at most once every 10 s.

**Consequences.** Nothing can be gamed or sorted by popularity. Any new feature that scores, ranks or profiles people goes against this decision.
